Drop your photos in this folder, then list them in config.js:

  photos:     shown in the "❤️ Us" card  (photo1.jpg, photo2.jpg, photo3.jpg by default)
  finalPhoto: shown above the final message (final.jpg by default)

Tip: phone photos are often 3–5 MB. Shrink them to about 1200px wide
so the game loads fast on mobile data.
